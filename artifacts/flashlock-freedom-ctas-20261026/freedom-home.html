Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Freedom different from other focus and screen time apps?

Freedom is the only app that can sync blocking sessions across all your devices. That means you can start a session on your desktop, but distracting apps on your mobile devices can be blocked too. Install Freedom wherever you need it, and enjoy some serious focus time.

We're devoted to pure focus. Freedom is designed to help you build better habits outside the app, not create another source of digital dependency. There are no dopamine-triggering streaks, points, leaderboards, or rewards. Every feature exists to reduce distractions and support real focus, not to keep you coming back to the app.

How much does Freedom cost?

Freedom is free to use with limited features. This is a great way to get started, and when you're ready to unlock the valuable Premium features, you'll have a variety of options. Premium is only $39.99 per year (equivalent to one coffee per month) – an incredible value considering you can add at least 2.5 hours of focused and productive time to your day.

Does Freedom block both apps and websites?

Absolutely. Customize your sessions to block any distraction, whether it's excessive tabs on your desktop browser or addictive apps like TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, news, games, shopping, or gambling on your mobile devices. Freedom efficiently blocks apps and websites across a range of devices including iPhones, iPads, Mac computers, Windows computers, Android devices, and Chrome devices.

What are some other cool Freedom features I should know about?

Delighted you asked. Besides syncing across multiple platforms, Freedom offers comprehensive app blocking on desktops, website exceptions (block-all except for specified sites), and immersive focus sounds and music - including pomodoro sessions and exclusive tracks from Brain.fm. Additionally, you'll find loads of resources aimed at helping you understand and overcome the challenge of distractions.
