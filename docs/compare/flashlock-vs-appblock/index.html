STUDY BREAKS

Choose FlashLock if…

You want a little study before more scrolling. You have cards to practise, or want to try a ready-made deck. You use Android and are happy to try a beta.

It adds a study step. That is different from setting a whole evening aside with blocked apps.

FEWER DISTRACTIONS

Choose AppBlock if…

You want distracting apps out of the way during homework or work. You prefer schedules, time limits or extra steps before changing your rules.

Need an iPhone app or browser extension? AppBlock has those too. Check its platform options.

Check before you install

Privacy: FlashLock stores cards and progress on your phone; optional usage sharing is off until enabled. AppBlock describes local core blocking, plus online features and data collection. Neither summary means “no data ever leaves your phone.” Read FlashLock’s policy and AppBlock’s Android policy.

Cost: AppBlock has a limited free plan and paid options. Check current prices and terms. For FlashLock, check the beta access details. We do not compare paid prices here.

Quick questions

Is FlashLock an AppBlock alternative?

Yes, for a different goal: flashcard practice rather than scheduled blocking.

Must every FlashLock answer be correct?

The public docs reviewed do not establish that. They describe a short review, then a return to your app.

Which is harder to bypass?

We have not tested that. AppBlock documents Strict Mode; that is not a head-to-head test.

Can I bring my own cards?

FlashLock supports making cards and importing Anki cards. We have not tested every deck format.
