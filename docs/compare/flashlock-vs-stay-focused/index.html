Choose cards or blocking profiles.

Choose FlashLock when the pause should contain revision. Choose Stay Focused when you want separate rules for work, sleep or study: profiles, daily limits, location rules and Pomodoro sessions. Its Strict Mode makes those blocks harder to change early.

This comparison is about Stay Focused by INNOXAPPS LLP, available for Android and iPhone—not the similarly named StayFocusd browser extension.
