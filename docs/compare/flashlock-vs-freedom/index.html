One phone or your whole workspace?

Choose Freedom if closing a phone app just sends you to the same distraction on a computer. Its sessions coordinate blocking across supported devices and can start on a recurring schedule. Choose FlashLock if the useful change is a few cards before a selected Android app.

For example, a protected writing session across a laptop and phone suits Freedom’s approach. Quick revision from a language deck before social media suits FlashLock’s. They solve different parts of a study routine.
