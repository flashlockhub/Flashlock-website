A review prompt or a focus session?

Choose FlashLock for small recall exercises when you reach for an app. Choose Forest when you have a task ready and want visual motivation to stay with it for a timed session. A growing tree measures time spent focusing, not which study cards you recalled.

Forest is not just a timer: its Android listing includes an app allowlist, scheduled Time Guard blocks and shared Plant Together sessions. FlashLock focuses on putting a card review into the app-opening moment.
