Privacy in FlashLock

Summary reviewed 30 October 2026.

This is a short overview, not a replacement for the full app privacy policy linked from Google Play.

Study data and community features

Study data is stored locally. Choosing to submit a community deck, send feedback or use an account also sends information to FlashLock’s services. Local storage does not mean every feature stays offline.

Permissions and optional sharing

Accessibility and Usage Access support selected-app learning breaks. You can revoke access in Android Settings. Optional product analytics starts off; account, security and SDK services are separate. ML Kit operational telemetry is not controlled by that analytics switch. Speech providers may process text online, depending on the voice.

Backups and deletion

Android backup depends on device settings. Signing in is not a FlashLock study-backup service. Uninstalling does not erase server records. See the account-deletion process, or contact hello@flashlock.app. A request is not confirmation of deletion.

This website

The website does not add advertising or visitor-analytics scripts. Its interactive card demo does not save study progress. GitHub Pages hosts the site and processes the requests needed to serve pages and files.

Google Play links include a fixed page or demo label. When you follow one, that label goes to Google to help measure referrals. The labels contain no personal identifiers, and this site does not send a separate tracking request.

iOS email signups

Signups are currently closed: the homepage does not accept an email submission. If you previously joined and want your details removed, contact hello@flashlock.app.
