Decide what the pause is for.

Choose FlashLock when you want to recall material from your own deck before continuing. Choose one sec when you mainly want a breathing or reflection prompt that helps you decide whether to open the app at all. Its current site also offers blocking, so it is more than a breathing timer.

one sec can include learning, too.

The LENGO integration adds vocabulary exercises before social media. Its official setup guide is for iOS; do not assume the same integration is on Android. For your own exam notes or imported flashcard deck, compare that workflow with FlashLock’s local imports.
