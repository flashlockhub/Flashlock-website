Study before scrolling on Android

FlashLock puts a short flashcard review before selected apps. A flashcard has a question and an answer: try recalling the answer before revealing it. Turn a familiar app-opening habit into a quick moment of learning.

Before you start

You’ll need an Android phone.

Quick setup

  1. Choose your apps. In setup, select an installed app you want a learning break before, then tap Continue. Start with one app and build from there.
  2. Choose a manageable card count. On How many flashcards before your app unlocks?, use the slider to select 1–50 cards, then Continue. Start small and find your rhythm. If asked to share app usage, Not now is available; sharing is optional.
  3. Review required permissions. On Accessibility, use Open Settings, select FlashLock and read Android’s confirmation before enabling it. Return to FlashLock. On Usage Access, open settings, choose FlashLock and enable Permit usage access. Accessibility shows the flashcards over chosen apps; Usage Access helps detect app use. Android labels vary by phone.
  4. Decide on optional settings. Notifications and Background activity are marked optional. Notifications offers Skip for now when off. Background activity explains unrestricted battery use; choose what suits your phone.
  5. Practise, then check your chosen app. Tap Continue to flashcards to practise in FlashLock. Fresh setup enables blocking for your choices. Afterwards, open the app you selected and check the actual learning break on your phone.

Use cards worth reviewing

Choose a deck with questions you understand. You can create your own or import an Anki deck. Check imported answers before using them. Keep the first break short, then adjust the card count to suit your routine.

If the break does not appear

Check that the app is selected, blocking is enabled, a deck contains cards, and your schedule is not paused. Recheck Accessibility and Usage Access. Setup help & video explains the permission route; if Accessibility remains disconnected, its help suggests turning FlashLock off and on in Android Accessibility settings, then returning. Background restrictions can also affect monitoring. Open your selected app again to try the break.
