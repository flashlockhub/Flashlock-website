Make room for what you’re learning.

Got a test coming up? Turn your notes into FlashLock cards or import Anki cards. Choose your apps and how many cards to review. Practise a little before you scroll, using the moments you already reach for your phone.

Want uninterrupted time across your phone and computer? Freedom syncs blocking sessions across devices, with advance and recurring schedules. FlashLock puts your study cards into app-opening breaks instead.
