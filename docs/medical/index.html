STILL THINKING

The questions med students ask.

How is this different from studying in Anki?

FlashLock adds a short review before selected Android apps. Importing an Anki file creates a separate FlashLock deck; that file-import route does not sync your Anki reviews.

Which medical deck should I use?

Start with material you are studying now. Create cards or import a deck, then check its answers against your course resources. The examples on this page are not a complete medical curriculum.

Does signing in back up my study progress?

Progress is saved on your phone. Community sign-in is not a FlashLock cloud-backup service. Keep a separate copy of important study material.
