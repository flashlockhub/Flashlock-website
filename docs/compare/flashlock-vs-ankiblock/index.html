Already reviewing in AnkiDroid? AnkiBlock uses that real study progress to unlock distracting apps. Smaller goals unlock one app; the full daily goal unlocks all blocked apps. Its listing describes it as 100% free, ad-free and open source.

FlashLock offers a different setup: create cards or import Anki cards and review inside FlashLock before opening selected apps. You do not need AnkiDroid. Anki import is not native AnkiDroid sync. Both apps connect study with app access; the choice is built-in reviews versus keeping your existing AnkiDroid workflow.
