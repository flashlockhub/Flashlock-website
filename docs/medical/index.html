STILL THINKING

The questions med students ask.

Why not just use Anki?

Anki is free and its spacing is great — but it’s the best flashcard app most students never open. FlashLock doesn’t ask you to visit a new app out of habit. It comes to you, inside the apps you’re already opening.

Is it really free?

Free during the beta — and the first 500 students keep it free forever. The 1,000-card first-year library is included with every account.

Can I import my own Anki decks?

Yes. Import an Anki deck from your device, preview the cards and add it as a local FlashLock deck. You can edit it without changing the original file.

Where do my cards live?

Your card text, decks and progress stay on your device. Optional usage analytics are off by default and never include card text or deck names.
