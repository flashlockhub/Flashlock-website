Make your next app opening a review.

FlashLock lets you practise your own material before selected apps open. Turn notes into cards or import Anki cards, then set a manageable card count for those small pauses.

Stay Focused by INNOXAPPS LLP is the Android app here, not the StayFocusd browser extension. Its profiles, daily limits and location rules suit planned boundaries. Strict Mode adds friction to changing blocks; Pomodoro sessions structure study time. Prefer that rule-based setup for longer work periods, or FlashLock when the pause itself should contain your cards.
