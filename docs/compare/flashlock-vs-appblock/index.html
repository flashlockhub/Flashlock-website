Choose the rule you want to follow.

Choose FlashLock when opening a familiar app should prompt a few cards from your course or language deck. Choose AppBlock when the goal is to keep apps or websites unavailable during a work period, after a usage limit or under another rule.

What AppBlock adds

On Android, Quick Block starts a one-off block; schedules can use time, usage, launch count, location or Wi-Fi. Strict Mode restricts changes to those blocks. AppBlock also offers an iPhone app, but its setup and features are not identical to Android.
